# EV Charging Etiquette and Trip Planning in India

## Planning Routes for EV Trips

### Pre-trip preparation

- Determine:
  - Distance, route options, and expected driving conditions.
- Identify:
  - Primary and backup charging stations along the way.
- Estimate:
  - Energy usage with realistic range assumptions.

### On-route flexibility

- Monitor:
  - State of charge and consumption as you drive.
- Adjust:
  - Speed and route if needed to maintain buffer to the next charger.

---

## Checking Charger Compatibility

### Connector and power

- Ensure:
  - The charger offers a connector compatible with your EV (e.g., CCS2, Type 2).
- Check:
  - Power rating and how it matches your vehicle’s maximum charging capability.

### App and access requirements

- Install:
  - Required apps or obtain RFID cards for networks on your route.
- Verify:
  - Account setup and payment methods before starting the trip.

---

## Charging Etiquette on Shared Infrastructure

### Charging only as much as needed

- At busy stations:
  - Avoid charging to 100% if a lower level is enough to reach your next planned stop with buffer.
- Priority:
  - Free up chargers for others once you have sufficient charge.

### Queueing and communication

- Respect:
  - Informal and formal queues at charging locations.
- If possible:
  - Coordinate with other users when arrival times are close and chargers are limited.

---

## Managing Wait Times and Reliability

### Handling wait times

- Anticipate:
  - Peak usage periods at popular chargers (weekends, holidays).
- Plan:
  - Alternative tasks during charging (meals, rest, work).

### Dealing with charger issues

- If a charger:
  - Is offline or malfunctioning, contact the network’s support line.
- Use:
  - Backup stations identified during trip planning.
- Maintain:
  - Sufficient SOC buffer to reach an alternative.

---

## Backup Options and Contingencies

### SOC buffers

- Avoid:
  - Arriving at chargers with extremely low SOC whenever possible.
- Maintain:
  - A reasonable buffer to account for unexpected detours or issues.

### Alternate routes and stops

- Identify:
  - Secondary chargers and alternate routes at planning stage.
- In emergencies:
  - Reduce speed and energy usage to stretch range if needed.

---

## Road-Trip Preparation Habits

### Vehicle checks

- Before departure:
  - Check tyre pressure, fluid levels, and charging cables.
- Ensure:
  - Software and navigation are updated.

### Packing and planning

- Pack:
  - Charging accessories allowed by your vehicle (AC cables, adapters where applicable).
- Plan:
  - Rest breaks aligned with charging stops for efficient use of time.

---

## Behaviour at Charging Locations

### Parking and movement

- Park:
  - Correctly within the bay without blocking other chargers.
- Move:
  - Your vehicle once charging is complete or sufficient.

### Respecting property and others

- Use:
  - Chargers and cables carefully.
- Dispose:
  - Of any waste properly and keep the area clean.

---

## Summary of Good Etiquette

- Plan:
  - Routes and backups thoughtfully.
- Charge:
  - Only as much as needed at busy stations.
- Communicate:
  - Politely and respect others waiting to charge.
- Prepare:
  - Your vehicle and accounts in advance for smoother trips.
