# Commercial Electric Vehicles and Electric Trucks in India

## Use Cases: Light-Duty and Medium-Duty

### Light-duty commercial EVs

- Examples:
  - Small delivery vans and pickups.
- Best suited for:
  - Urban and suburban deliveries, short to medium routes.
- Priorities:
  - Low running cost, maneuverability, and easy charging.

### Medium-duty electric trucks

- Examples:
  - Larger rigid trucks for intra-city or short intercity routes.
- Best suited for:
  - High-volume routes with predictable patterns.
- Priorities:
  - Payload efficiency, reliable charging, and uptime.

---

## Delivery Operations and Route Planning

### Defining routes

- Fixed routes:
  - Simplify planning of range and charging.
- Variable routes:
  - Require careful buffer planning and more flexible charging options.

### Charging downtime and scheduling

- Overnight depot charging:
  - Primary strategy for many fleets.
- Midday top-ups:
  - At depots, hubs, or public chargers if needed.
- Scheduling:
  - Routes must be planned to align driving and charging windows.

---

## Payload Trade-Offs and Vehicle Design

### Payload vs battery weight

- Larger batteries:
  - Increase vehicle weight, potentially reducing payload capacity.
- Balance:
  - Between carrying capacity and required range must be carefully considered.

### Body and configuration

- Options:
  - Closed bodies, refrigerated units, open decks.
- Impact:
  - Body type influences weight, aerodynamics, and energy use.

---

## Fleet Economics and Operating Cost

### Running cost structure

- Energy:
  - Electricity cost per km is typically lower than diesel.
- Maintenance:
  - Reduced engine-related maintenance; focus shifts to tyres, brakes, and suspension.
- Driver:
  - Smooth and quiet EV operation can reduce fatigue.

### Cost-benefit over time

- High utilization:
  - Increases the value of lower running cost.
- Depreciation:
  - Needs to be accounted for, alongside potential policy incentives.

---

## Serviceability and Support

### Maintenance infrastructure

- Depot capability:
  - Basic checks and routine tasks can be done in-house for some fleets.
- OEM service:
  - Must be available and reliable for high-voltage and major repairs.

### Training and safety

- Staff training:
  - For safe handling of high-voltage systems.
- Safety protocols:
  - For charging, maintenance, and emergency situations.

---

## Where Electric Trucks Make Sense Today

### Strong use cases

- Urban distribution:
  - Short, repeatable routes with overnight depot charging.
- Last-mile delivery:
  - High stop density and frequent braking favour regen.
- Areas with:
  - Good access to industrial or depot power for charging.

### Emerging but limited use cases

- Short intercity operations:
  - Where routes align with existing or planned charging infrastructure.
- Operations:
  - Willing to build their own private fast-charging nodes.

---

## Current Limitations and Challenges

### Range and charging infrastructure

- Long-haul:
  - Still challenging for full-electric trucks due to range and charging-time constraints.
- Infrastructure:
  - Limited DC fast-charging capacity designed specifically for heavy CVs.

### Upfront cost and financing

- Higher purchase price:
  - Compared to diesel analogs.
- Financing:
  - Requires TCO-focused evaluation and sometimes specialized loan products.

---

## Practical Checklist for Fleet Operators

### Technical suitability

- Routes:
  - Clear understanding of daily distance and back-to-base patterns.
- Charging:
  - Confirm depot electrical capacity and any public charging support.
- Payload:
  - Ensure EV truck’s payload meets operational needs after accounting for battery weight.

### Economic evaluation

- Calculate:
  - Fuel vs electricity cost over expected life.
- Include:
  - Maintenance, downtime, driver productivity, and policy incentives.
- Scenario test:
  - High and low utilization cases.

---

## Decision Summary

- Commercial EVs and trucks in India:
  - Work best in structured operations with predictable routes and strong depot charging.
- Operators:
  - Need to balance payload, range, and economics carefully.
- As infrastructure and technology mature:
  - The viable envelope for electric trucks will expand, but current deployments are most effective in targeted use cases rather than all scenarios.
